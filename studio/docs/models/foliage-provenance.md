# Canopy texture provenance

`public/assets/models/evergreen-spray.png` is a single image-generated RGBA leaf spray, created for this project. It is instanced as spatially distributed, alpha-tested cards around modelled branches. The asset is unchanged from the generation output (1254 × 1254).

Prompt: A photorealistic, isolated small branching spray of coast-live-oak/olive-like leaves on genuinely transparent alpha. Approximately 35 pointed oval, muted olive leaves with thin brown twigs, visible veins and varied orientations. Roughly round centered arrangement, clear margins and many gaps. Neutral diffuse lighting, minimal baked shadows and highlights. No whole tree, trunk, ground, background, cast shadow, text, watermark or border.

Other surface maps are deterministic procedural base-color, roughness, normal and ambient-occlusion maps. Reference images are preserved unchanged.
